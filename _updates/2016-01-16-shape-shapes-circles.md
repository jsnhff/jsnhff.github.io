Circles is up at <a href="http://www.shapeshapes.com/">Shape Shapes</a>, Seokhoon Choi's single-page gallery for artists' shapes — one show at a time, the whole exhibition an overlay on one web page. Mine came out of a long stretch of <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/BDeWisFqgNl/">research into coins</a>. I 3D printed a few of them later that year and sold them at the Internet Yami-Ichi.
