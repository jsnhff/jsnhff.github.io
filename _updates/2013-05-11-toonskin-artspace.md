<a href="/projects/endless-opportunities/" title="Endless Opportunities">Endless Opportunities</a> is included in Toonskin, curated by Kenya Robinson at Artspace in New Haven — May 11 to June 30, 2013. The show brought together nineteen artists reinterpreting Blackness, and otherness more broadly, within an animated context, reading sequential art against the minstrel underpinnings of Tom and Jerry and Mickey Mouse.
