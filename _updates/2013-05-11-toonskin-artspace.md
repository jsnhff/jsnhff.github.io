<a href="/projects/endless-opportunities/" title="Endless Opportunities">Endless Opportunities</a> is included in <a href="https://artspacenewhaven.org/exhibitions/toonskin/" title="Toonskin at Artspace New Haven">Toonskin</a>, curated by Kenya Robinson at <a href="https://artspacenewhaven.org" title="Artspace New Haven">Artspace</a> in New Haven — May 11 to June 30, 2013. The show brought together nineteen artists reinterpreting Blackness, and otherness more broadly, within an animated context, reading sequential art against the minstrel underpinnings of Tom and Jerry and Mickey Mouse.
