I showed work at BYOB Cincy on November 4, 2011 — a one-night bring-your-own-beamer exhibition in the lobby and lower level of the Lois &amp; Richard Rosenthal Center for Contemporary Art, organized by Jordan Tate, Aaron Walker, and Chris Collins. Everyone hauled in their own projector. Clement Valla came out from Providence with me.
