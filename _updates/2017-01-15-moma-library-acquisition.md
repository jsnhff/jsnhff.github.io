American Psycho and AutoSummarize are now held in the <a href="https://www.moma.org/research/library">MoMA Library</a> at The Museum of Modern Art, New York, through its acquisition of the <a href="https://soulellis.com/entries/lotpw.html">Library of the Printed Web</a> — a collection of 244 items by 130 artists curated by Paul Soulellis.
