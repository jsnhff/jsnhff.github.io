<a href="/projects/endless-opportunities/" title="Endless Opportunities">Endless Opportunities</a> is included in <a href="https://www.linkartcenter.eu/archives/2120" title="Collect the WWWorld at 319 Scholes">Collect the WWWorld: The Artist as Archivist in the Internet Age</a>, curated by <a href="http://domenicoquaranta.com/" title="Domenico Quaranta">Domenico Quaranta</a> at <a href="http://319scholes.org/" title="319 Scholes">319 Scholes</a> in Brooklyn — October 18 to November 4, 2012, alongside JODI, Jon Rafman, Ryan Trecartin, Penelope Umbrico, Clement Valla, Oliver Laric, Constant Dullaart, and Eva & Franco Mattes. The exhibition began at Spazio Contemporanea in Brescia in 2011; the catalogue is published by Link Editions.
