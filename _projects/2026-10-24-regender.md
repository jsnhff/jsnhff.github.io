  Literature carries invisible gender assumptions that shape how we read, and what we see. Experiencing the same story through different perspectives reveals the narrative patterns and power structures woven into it.

  Jane Austen's *Pride & Prejudice* (1813), run through free and open source software that systematically transforms gendered language in books. Four complete volumes: genders swapped, all male, all female, and gender neutral.

  The software is [regender.xyz](https://regender.xyz). Its [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/jsnhff/regender-xyz), under the GPL: use it, fork it, run it on another book.
